    Love brains, flies and chromatin, and want to join us?

    Postdoctoral positions

    Fellowships for postdoctoral work are available from organisations such as the NHMRC, Alzheimer’s Australia, EMBO and Human Frontiers. Please contact Owen if you’re interested in joining the lab.

    PhD positions

    Please contact Owen to talk through research project options if you’re interested in joining the lab. PhD projects are pretty flexible, so provided you’re interested in some aspect of fly brains, chromatin and transcription, we can probably work something out.

    Scholarship funding for PhD candidates is available through the Menzies Institute and University of Tasmania. In addition to the normal PhD scholarships, Menzies has access to some elite scholarships available for exceptional high-ranking applicants, that come with an extra $7500 top-up and substantial additional conference and workshop travel money over the course of your studies.

    Honours degrees

    Interested in doing Honours with us? You’d be amazed at just how fly research you can fit into an Honours research year! Menzies has scholarships for Honours students, too, which can make the year a little easier financially. Come talk to us to learn more.
